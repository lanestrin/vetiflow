## Goal

On the ER board, typing in the top bar's "Search patients…" narrows the board
to the matching cards straight away, as in the prototype. Staff can still
reach a patient who isn't on the board through a "Search all patients" link to
the Patients search (4.6a).

## Design reference

`blueprint/reference/vetiflow-er-dashboard.html`: the top bar's `.search`
input. Its script hides every `.patient` card whose text doesn't contain the
typed text (case-insensitive) and shows them all again when the field is empty.
The prototype has no "no match" state or link to Patients; those follow its
`.tiny` and `#flowFoot` style.

## In scope

- **Shared search text:** `AppShell` holds the top bar's search text (today
  it's local state in `TopBar`) and shares it through a `ShellSearchContext`
  (`components/shell/shellSearchContext.ts`, like `toastContext.ts`).
  `useShellSearch()` returns the text, or `""` outside the shell.
- **Board matching:** on `/er`, a card shows when the trimmed, case-insensitive
  text is found in its patient name, case number ("ER-2481"), owner name,
  species, breed or presenting complaint. An empty search shows every card.
  The rule is `matchesSearch(emergencyCase, text)` in `boardFormat.ts`.
- **Combined with the stat card filter (2.12b):** the search applies on top of
  it. Lane counts are the cards shown.
- **Foot while searching:** "Showing N matching “<text>” ·" followed by a
  **Search all patients** link to `/records?search=<text>`, so a patient who
  isn't on the board can still be found. With no matches, each lane shows its
  usual "No patients".
- **Enter** still opens the Patients search with the text and clears the
  field, as today, which also clears the board's search.
- **Selection:** a selected card hidden by the search stays selected, and the
  patient overview stays open.

## Out of scope

- The Incoming toggle (2.14b), the Filter menu (2.14c) and Priority (2.14d).
- Searching patients who aren't on the board from the board itself; the link
  hands that to Patients (4.6a).
- The phone top bar, where the search box is already a link to Patients
  (2.10b). Board search is for wider screens, as the box is.
- Keeping the search text when leaving `/er`, or clearing it then. It behaves
  as the box does today.

## Notes for the AI

- Match on the fields above, not on the card's rendered text. That's the same
  idea as the prototype, but it doesn't depend on markup or wording.
- Keep the filtering in `PatientFlowBoard`, next to the stat card filter, so
  the foot can describe both.
- The context's default value is `""`, so pages and tests outside the shell
  just see no search.
