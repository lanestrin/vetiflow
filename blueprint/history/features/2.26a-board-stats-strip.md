## Goal

The four large stat cards above the ER board become the prototype's compact stats strip: In care, Awaiting triage, Critical and Critical without vet. Each is a number and a label, and each filters the board. The board's footer always says how many active patients are shown out of all active patients, so staff never mistake a filtered board for the whole department.

## In scope

- **The strip** replaces the four cards in `BoardStats`. It keeps `<section aria-label="Board summary">`, with four toggle buttons (`aria-pressed`) in this order:

  | Stat | Value | Filter when pressed |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | In care | `summary.patientsInCare` | every active case (`all`) |
  | Awaiting triage | `summary.awaitingTriage` | stage Incoming (`awaitingTriage`, as today) |
  | Critical | `summary.criticalPatients` | acuity Critical (`critical`, as today) |
  | Critical without vet | counted from the board list: acuity Critical and no `assignedDoctor` | the same cases (new `criticalWithoutVet`) |

  - Each button's accessible name is its label and value, for example "Critical without vet, 2".
  - Without a summary, the first three show "—", as the cards do today. The fourth always has a value, because the board list has loaded.
- **The pressed stat:**
  - It has a visible cue that is not only colour: a 3 px teal bar under the stat and a bold label.
  - The "Active" pill goes.
  - Critical without vet uses the urgent red text token for its number, as in the prototype; its label carries the meaning.
- **Empty filters:** pressing Awaiting triage with nobody waiting keeps today's toast ("No patients awaiting triage") and leaves the board unchanged. Pressing Critical without vet when the count is 0 does the same, with the toast "No critical patients without a veterinarian".
- **Removed:**
  - Door to doctor (until 2.16b).
  - The card footers: arrivals since the shift began, oldest triage wait, and the care-team sentence.
  - The rows still show "Waiting N min" for incoming cases (2.25), and the heading keeps the shift chip.
- **Footer count:** the board's footer always reads "Showing N of M active patients". N is the matching active patients after the stat filter, the Filter menu and the search; M is every active patient on the board. One short note is appended when a stat filter is on:

  | Filter | Note |
  | --- | --- |
  | Awaiting triage | "· awaiting triage · initial priority is provisional" |
  | Critical | "· critical only" |
  | Critical without vet | "· critical without a veterinarian" |

  - With a search: "Showing N of M active patients matching “q” · Search all patients" (the existing link).
- **No pagination:** the board stays one scrolling list in the existing fixed acuity order.
- **Phones:**
  - At 650 px and below the strip is a 2×2 grid with no dividers between rows, as in the prototype.
  - Each stat is at least 44 px tall.
  - No horizontal scroll at 390 px and 320 px.
