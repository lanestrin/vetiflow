## Goal

Staff open **New intake** on the ER board, fill the prototype's "New patient
intake" dialog and press **Add to triage queue**; the pet appears in its
lane as New and is selected.

## Design reference

`blueprint/reference/vetiflow-er-dashboard.html`: the `#intakeModal` dialog
(`.modalback`, `.modal` 700 px, `.modalhead` with "Patient flow" eyebrow,
"New patient intake" and the hint, `.formgrid` two columns, `.field`,
`.severity` radios, `.modalnote`, `.modalfoot` with Cancel and Add to triage
queue) and the heading's `＋ New intake` button (`.btn`).

## In scope

- A **＋ New intake** button in the board's heading (2.15b restyles the
  heading around it).
- The dialog, field for field as in the prototype, with its wording and
  placeholders; focus starts on Patient name; Escape, Cancel, × and a backdrop
  click close it; focus returns to the button.
- Required fields checked before sending (Patient name, Species, Pet parent /
  guardian, Phone number, Chief complaint, Initial priority); the server's
  field errors shown under their fields; other failures as one message.
  Nothing typed is lost on an error.
- **Add to triage queue** is disabled while saving ("Adding…"). On success:
  the dialog closes and clears, the board reloads, the new card is selected,
  and the toast says "Toby added to triage queue".
- Field edges darkened to 3:1 (`#8f9593`) and the note to AA, as for other
  prototype colours.

## Out of scope

- Duplicate warnings (4.7), Complete triage (4.2), the heading's other parts
  (2.15b).
