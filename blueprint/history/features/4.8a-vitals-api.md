## Goal

Staff can record a new set of vitals, now including SpO₂, and a pain score
(0 to 10) on a case, with who and when. The API says which readings are
outside the alert ranges, so the panel can turn them red (4.8b), and returns
the latest pain score on the case detail and the board list.

## In scope

- **SpO₂:** `VitalsRecord.SpO2` (`int?`, percent), with a check constraint
  `0 to 100` and a migration. Returned on `VitalsRecordDto` as `spO2`.
- **Record vitals:** `POST /api/cases/{id}/vitals` with
  `{ heartRate?, respiratoryRate?, temperatureF?, spO2?, mucousMembraneColor?, capillaryRefillTime?, mentation? }`.
  It saves the set through `EmergencyCase.RecordVitals` (never changes
  acuity), with `RecordedByStaffId` from the signed-in account and
  `RecordedAt` from the server, and returns `201` with the saved
  `VitalsRecordDto` (including `outOfRange`).
- **Alert ranges (demo, provisional):** one static `VitalRanges` class in
  `vetiflow.Server/Models/` holds the ranges by species, and
  `VitalRanges.OutOfRange(species, vitals)` returns the names of the readings
  outside them. Every `VitalsRecordDto` (detail list and the `201` response)
  gains `outOfRange`: a list of `heartRate`, `respiratoryRate`, `temperatureF`
  or `spO2`, empty when all are in range or missing. The ranges below are
  demo values, not clinic values, and are replaced before real clinic data
  (decision "Vital alert ranges" stays open for the clinic's numbers):

  | Species | Heart rate (bpm) | Resp rate (/min) | Temp | SpO₂ |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Dog | 60 to 140 | 10 to 30 | 37.5 to 39.2 °C (99.5 to 102.6 °F) | 95% or more |
  | Cat | 140 to 220 | 20 to 40 | 37.8 to 39.2 °C (100.0 to 102.6 °F) | 95% or more |
  | Anything else | no flags | no flags | no flags | no flags |

  Species matches `Patient.Species` ignoring case (it is free text today).
  The bounds are inclusive. With these, the prototype's Milo (dog, 148 bpm,
  48 /min, 38.9 °C, 91%) flags heart rate, resp rate and SpO₂, as the
  prototype shows.
- **Pain score:** a new `PainScore` entity (`Id`, `EmergencyCaseId`, `Score`
  0 to 10, `Notes?`, `RecordedAt`, `RecordedByStaffId?`), with a check
  constraint `Score BETWEEN 0 AND 10`, FK with `Restrict`, an index on
  `(EmergencyCaseId, RecordedAt)`, RLS and `AppRoleAccess.Grant`, in a
  migration. `EmergencyCase.RecordPainScore` adds it and sets `LastUpdated`;
  it leaves acuity alone.
- **Record pain score:** `POST /api/cases/{id}/pain-scores` with
  `{ score, notes? }`, `201` with the saved score.
- **Validation (400 ProblemDetails, field errors name the field, never the
  value):**
  - vitals: at least one reading or observation given; `heartRate` 1 to 400;
    `respiratoryRate` 1 to 200; `temperatureF` 80.0 to 115.0; `spO2` 50 to
    100; the three observation texts trimmed, at most 50 characters each;
  - pain: `score` required, 0 to 10; `notes` trimmed, at most 500 characters.
- **Refusals:** `404` when no case has that id; `409` when the case is
  discharged, as triage and tasks do.
- **Live board:** each write sets `LastUpdated` and sends `caseUpdated`.
- **Reads:** the case detail and the board list each add `latestPainScore`:
  - detail: `{ id, score, notes, recordedAt, recordedBy: { id, name } | null } | null`;
  - list: `{ score, recordedAt } | null` (no notes on the board list).
- **Seed:** the demo cases get an SpO₂ on their existing vitals and one or two
  pain scores, so the panel and `/check` have something to show.

## Recommended defaults (taken with the spec's OK, listed for review)

- **The demo ranges above.** You chose demo ranges for now. They live in one
  class so the clinic's numbers replace them in one place.
- **Temperature stays in °F** in storage and in the API, as today. 4.8b's form
  takes °C and converts, as the tiles already do. Ranges are kept in °F in
  code, with the °C value in a comment.
- **Plausibility limits** on recorded vitals (HR 1 to 400, RR 1 to 200, temp
  80 to 115 °F, SpO₂ 50 to 100) and the 50- and 500-character text limits: the
  standards require bounds on every field; these refuse typos, not real
  readings.
- **No timeline events** for vitals or pain scores, as for tasks. The record
  itself carries who and when.
- **Latest pain score only** on the detail and list; no history endpoint.

## Out of scope

- The SpO₂ tile value, the red alert style, recording vitals from the panel,
  the pain meter and the **Record score** dialog (4.8b).
- The clinic's real ranges, and ranges for species other than dog and cat.
- Editing or deleting vitals or pain scores.
- Vitals on the board list, and "SpO₂ · latest" in My patients (2.22).
- Roles: any team member may write, until S.6.

## Data / contracts

- **`VitalsRecords.SpO2`:** `integer NULL`, check `"SpO2" BETWEEN 0 AND 100`.
- **`PainScores`:** `Id uuid`, `EmergencyCaseId uuid` (FK, Restrict),
  `Score integer` (check 0 to 10), `Notes text NULL`, `RecordedAt timestamptz`,
  `RecordedByStaffId uuid NULL` (FK, Restrict); index
  `(EmergencyCaseId, RecordedAt)`; RLS on and `vetiflow_app_rows` policy.
- **`VitalsRecordDto`** adds `spO2: number | null` and
  `outOfRange: ("heartRate" | "respiratoryRate" | "temperatureF" | "spO2")[]`.
  Later items keep these names.
- **`POST /api/cases/{id}/vitals`:** body as above; `201` with
  `VitalsRecordDto`; `400`, `401`, `403`, `404` or `409`.
- **`POST /api/cases/{id}/pain-scores`:** body `{ score, notes? }`; `201` with
  `{ id, score, notes, recordedAt, recordedBy }`; `400`, `401`, `403`, `404`
  or `409`.
- **Detail** adds `latestPainScore: { id, score, notes, recordedAt, recordedBy } | null`.
- **List** adds `latestPainScore: { score, recordedAt } | null`.
- **Hub:** `caseUpdated(caseId)` after each write, the existing message.

## Security

- Both routes require the team member policy. Pet parents get `403` and never
  see vitals or pain scores; the owner page is unchanged.
- Who recorded it comes from the signed-in account, never the request body.
- Pain notes and observations are clinical text: never logged, errors name
  the field only. The board list carries the score and time, not the notes.
- The new table gets RLS and the app role grant like every other table.
