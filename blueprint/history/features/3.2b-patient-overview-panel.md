## Goal

Selecting a card on the ER board opens the prototype's **Patient overview**
panel beside the board, filled from `GET /api/cases/{id}` (3.2a), so staff see
one case's essentials without leaving the board.

## Design reference

`blueprint/reference/vetiflow-er-dashboard.html`, `aside.detail` and `.layout`:

- `.layout`: board and panel side by side (`minmax(530px,1.45fr)
  minmax(350px,.85fr)`, 16 px gap); at 1050 px the panel stacks under the
  board as a two-column grid (header and actions full width); at 650 px it is
  one column.
- `.detailhead`: "Patient overview", "Case ER-2481 · Checked in 10:42 AM", the
  **•••** tool button.
- `.patienthero`: `.petavatar` (🐕 on a warm tint for dogs), `.heroname`,
  `.herometa` "Dog · Golden Retriever · 8 years · 31.4 kg", the acuity badge
  on the right.
- `.ownerbar`: "Pet parent", the name, Call and Message.
- `.vitals`: four `.vital` tiles (Heart rate, Resp rate, Temp, SpO₂).
- The `📍 Location` row ("Treatment · Oxygen cage").
- `.section` **Care timeline**: `.event` entries (name, time, description),
  `.event.pending` with a hollow dot.
- `.section` **Next actions** with **＋ Add task**: `.task` rows with `.check`
  (✓ when done), the label and the assignee.
- `.detailactions`: **⇄ Handoff** and **Update status**.

## In scope

- **Fetching:** `getCase(id)` in `casesApi.ts` and a `CaseDetail` type for the
  3.2a response. The panel loads the selected case, and loads it again each
  time the board reloads (any `caseUpdated` from the hub, or a new intake). It
  keeps the shown case on screen while a reload of the same case runs.
- **States:**
  - Nothing selected: "Select a patient on the board to see their overview."
  - Loading a newly selected case: "Loading case…" (`aria-busy`).
  - `404`: "This case is no longer on the board."
  - Other failure: "Unable to load this case." with **Try again**.
  - `401` and `403` behave as on the board (sign-in with the session-ended
    notice, or the pet parent home).
  - A selected case that leaves the board list is unselected (empty state).
- **Header:** "Patient overview"; "Case ER-2481 · Checked in 10:42 AM" (the
  date is added when check-in was not today: "Checked in 27 Oct, 10:42 PM").
- **Hero:** a decorative species avatar (🐕 dog, 🐈 cat, 🐾 otherwise); the
  name as the panel's patient heading; "Dog · Golden Retriever · 8 years ·
  31.4 kg" (age in years, months under one year; parts that are missing are
  left out); the `AcuityBadge`, with "· provisional" for an incoming case.
- **Pet parent bar:** "Pet parent" and the owner's name, or "No pet parent
  recorded". No phone or email on screen.
- **Vitals tiles** from the newest vitals record: "148 bpm", "48 /min",
  "38.9 °C" (converted from the stored °F, one decimal); "Not recorded" for any
  missing reading. SpO₂ is always "Not recorded" until 4.8a adds it.
- **Location:** "<area> · <room name>" (area from the room type: Exam,
  Treatment, ICU, Oxygen, Isolation, Surgery, Imaging), or "No room assigned".
- **Care timeline:** the case events oldest first: a staff label per event
  type ("Arrived", "Triage completed", "Stage changed", …), the time, and the
  description with who did it. Open blockers follow as **pending** items
  (hollow dot, "Waiting" in place of a time). Empty: "No events yet."
- **Next actions:** tasks of type Task (not cancelled), open first: the check
  box (✓ and done styling when done), the description, and the assignee's name
  or "Pending" when open and unassigned. Empty: "No tasks yet."
- **Controls shown, not wired:** **•••**, Call, Message, **＋ Add task**,
  **⇄ Handoff** and **Update status** render as disabled buttons. Their items
  wire them: 4.2b and 4.3b (**•••**), 6.4b (Call, Message), 4.4b (Add task),
  6.1b (Handoff), 4.9b (Update status).
- **Build plan:** the 4.8b line says the tiles already exist from 3.2b and
  4.8b adds SpO₂, the alert style and recording.

## Out of scope

- **Complete triage** (4.2b); Next reassessment and Client update rows (4.4b);
  the pain score meter (4.8b); vitals alert colours and SpO₂ values (4.8);
  clinical tags (3.5b); "Full record ↗" (no full record page exists yet).
- Selecting a card by default: the panel starts empty until a card is chosen.
- Any server change.

## Data / contracts

- No API change. The client reads `GET /api/cases/{id}` as 3.2a returns it:
  `caseNumber`, `patient { name, species, breed, dateOfBirth,
  dateOfBirthIsEstimated, … }`, `owner { firstName, lastName, phone, email }`
  or null, `weightKg`, `stage`, `acuity`, `arrivalTime`, `room { id, name,
  type }` or null, `vitals` (newest first), `tasks` (open first), `timeline`
  (oldest first). Fields the panel does not show yet (allergies, medications,
  authorization, referral) are typed but unused.
- Enum values arrive camelCase (`NameOnlyEnumConverter`); labels live in the
  client.

## Security

- No new surface. The detail endpoint stays team members only (401 signed out,
  403 pet parent); the panel follows the board's handling of both.
- The panel shows the owner's name only; phone and email stay off screen until
  6.4b's Call and Message. Nothing from the detail is logged to the console.
- Pet parents never reach the board or this panel.

## Notes for the AI

- Prototype text colours below AA are darkened just enough, as in 2.11b
  (muted text uses `--color-shell-muted`).
- Disabled controls: native `disabled` buttons; no toasts or "coming soon"
  text.
- Acuity is read only; the panel never changes it.
- Use the 2.11b `useNow` tick for "today" in the check-in label.

## Decisions taken for this item (recommended defaults)

- **Pending on the timeline** = open blockers, since events have no pending
  state. Open tasks stay in Next actions.
- **Unwired controls** are shown disabled until their items.
- **No default selection:** the panel starts empty (the prototype preselects
  the first card).
- **Temperature** is shown in °C from 3.2b, as the prototype does.
