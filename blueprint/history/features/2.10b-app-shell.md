## Goal

Every staff page sits inside the ER Command Center's frame: the navy rail on
the left and the white top bar with the breadcrumb, search, bell and profile.
Staff move between the board and Patients from the rail and sign out from the
profile.

## Design reference

`blueprint/reference/vetiflow-er-dashboard.html`:

- `.rail` (76 px navy, `.brandmark` "V", `.navico` 44 px with the active
  marker bar, Settings at the bottom) and its six icons.
- `.topbar` (74 px, `.crumb` "VetiFlow / **ER Command Center**", `.search`
  218 px "Search patients…", the bell, `.profile` with `.avatar` and name).
- `.toast` (navy, bottom centre).
- At 650 px: rail 56 px, icons 40 px, top bar 62 px with 14 px padding, the
  search shrinks to its icon and the profile shows only the avatar.

## In scope

- **Client profile:** `SignedInUser` adds `displayName` and `initials` from
  `GET /api/auth/me` (2.10a).
- **AppShell** around `/er`, `/records` and four new "coming soon" routes:
  - Rail links: Overview `/overview`, ER board `/er`, Patients `/records`,
    Team `/team`, Reports `/reports`, Settings `/settings`. Each has an
    accessible name and `aria-current` on the active one.
  - Breadcrumb: "VetiFlow / <page>": ER Command Center, Patients, Overview,
    Team, Reports, Settings.
  - Search: Enter opens Patients with that search (`/records?search=…`).
    Filtering the board as you type is 2.14. At 650 px the field collapses to
    an icon link to Patients.
  - Bell: a button "Notifications"; pressing it shows the toast "No
    notifications yet". No unread dot until 2.17.
  - Profile: a button with the avatar initials and display name; it opens a
    menu with **Sign out** (Escape or a click outside closes it). Sign out
    clears the token and goes to sign-in, as `SessionBar` does now.
- **Toast:** `ToastProvider` and `useToast()` in the shell; one message at a
  time, about 2.2 s, announced politely (`role="status"`).
- **Coming soon page** for Overview, Team, Reports and Settings: the page name
  as the heading and a calm line saying it is on the way, with links to the
  ER board and Patients.
- The board and records pages lose their own sign-out bar and cross links
  (the rail replaces them). The pet parent page keeps `SessionBar`.

## Out of scope

- Board content and its heading area (2.11b, 2.15b); board search and filter
  (2.14); notifications (2.17); the pages behind the rail (M4).
- The prototype's fonts (DM Sans, Manrope): adding them needs a font package
  or a font host. Follow-up item, below.
