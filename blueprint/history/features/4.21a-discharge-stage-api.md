## Goal

Moving a case to the Discharge stage through Update status records its discharge time,
removes it from the ER board, and frees its room. The change is written to the timeline
with the signed-in staff member as the actor. A discharged case then takes no further
triage, task, vitals or status changes.

## Approved policy

Decided by the person on 2026-11-01 (recorded in the build plan's Decisions as
"Discharge leaves the board"):

- Moving a case to the Discharge stage is a discharge, not a clinical departure. It
  supersedes "readiness is not departure" for the Discharge stage only.
- Departure, admission, transfer and death outcomes stay with 6.2e and are not changed here.

Defaults in this spec, to confirm at approval:

- The case keeps its room on the record (`RoomId` stays). The room is freed because the
  room index covers only active cases, so no room is cleared.
- A move to Discharge writes two timeline entries: `StageChanged` (as now) and
  `Discharged`, both with the actor.
- A discharged case refuses further status and next-action changes too, with 409, as
  triage, tasks and vitals already do.

## In scope

- Moving a case to Discharge through `PATCH /api/cases/{id}/status` (4.9a) sets
  `DischargedAt` once, to the time of the move.
- The `Discharged` timeline entry, with the actor.
- A discharged case refuses status and next-action changes (409).
- The demo seed gives its Discharge-stage cases a discharge time, so the seeded board
  matches the rule.

## Out of scope

- The Discharges page (4.21b).
- Clearing or changing the room on the case, and room assignment (4.3).
- Departure, admission, transfer and death outcomes (6.2e, 6.2f).
- Any change to the client. The board, the Update status dialog and the panel already
  read `DischargedAt` and the stage from the server.
- A migration: `DischargedAt` already exists. Existing rows are not backfilled; the
  demo reseeds, and no clinic data is in the system yet (build plan security gates).

## Data / contracts

- No migration, no new column, no new enum value (`CaseEventType.Discharged` exists).
- Endpoint unchanged: `PATCH /api/cases/{id}/status`. Its 409 gains one reason: "This case has been discharged."
- Board list and summary already filter on `DischargedAt == null`, so they change with no code.
- Keep for later: `DischargedAt` is the discharge record. 6.2e's departure rules may add a separate
  departure time; they must not change what a discharge means here.

## Security

- Unchanged: the team-member policy on the status endpoint. Any team member may discharge a case
  until S.6 restricts stage changes by role. Owners cannot reach it.
- The timeline entry text is the stage and status names only; it contains no clinical text.
- Never log the next action text or the discharge notes.
