## Goal

On phones (700 px and below) staff move between pages from a bar at the bottom of the screen, within thumb reach and always visible, instead of opening the top bar's menu button first. Destinations that do not fit comfortably sit behind a More menu in that bar. This replaces 2.23's phone menu.

## Design reference

- **`blueprint/reference/er-board-poc/VetiFlow-ER-Prototype.html`** (the canonical prototype; read it beside this spec while building):
  - **Markup:** `<nav class="rail" aria-label="Main navigation">` with icon buttons that each carry a `.nav-label`.
  - **Styles (`@media(max-width:700px)`):**
    - `.rail{top:auto; bottom:0; left:0; right:0; width:100%; height:calc(62px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom)); padding:5px 8px calc(5px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom)); flex-direction:row; justify-content:space-around; gap:5px; border-top:1px solid #31515b}`
    - `.rail .logo{display:none}`
    - `.rail button{width:56px; height:48px; padding:5px 2px; flex-direction:column; align-items:center; justify-content:center; gap:2px; border-radius:8px}` and `.rail svg{width:19px; height:19px}`
    - `.nav-label{display:block; font-size:9px; font-weight:500; line-height:14px}`
    - `main{padding-bottom:calc(80px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom))}` so content clears the bar.
    - `.toast{bottom:calc(80px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom))}` so toasts sit above the bar.
- Decisions in `blueprint/build-plan.md`: **Navigation** (the app keeps Overview, ER board, Patients, Team, Reports and Settings; on phones a bottom bar shows them, with an overflow menu for destinations that do not fit comfortably; the prototype's reduced navigation does not replace the app's).

## In scope

- **Above 700 px:** the side rail as on desktop today, down to 701 px. The top bar's menu button and the slide-in rail from 2.23 are removed at every width. The top bar's other 760 px rules (the breadcrumb taking the free space, the right-hand group pushed to the end) stay.
- **700 px and below, the bottom bar:**
  - The same `<nav aria-label="Main">`, fixed to the bottom of the viewport, full width, the prototype's height, padding, border and safe-area inset.
  - Five items, left to right: Overview, ER board, Patients, Team, More. Each shows its icon above its label. Items are at least 44 px tall and wide.
  - The current page's item shows the active style and keeps `aria-current="page"`.
  - The brand mark is hidden.
- **The More menu:**
  - A button named "More" with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, opening a small menu above the bar with links to Reports and Settings (icon and label, at least 44 px tall).
  - More shows the active style while the current page is Reports or Settings.
  - Choosing a link navigates and closes the menu. Escape closes it and returns focus to More. A press outside it closes it. Any navigation (search, browser history) closes it, as 2.23's menu did.
  - Crossing above 700 px closes it.
- **Content clearance:** the shell's main column gets the prototype's bottom padding at 700 px and below, so the last row of every page can be scrolled clear of the bar. Toasts sit above the bar.
- No horizontal page scroll at 700, 390 and 320 px; the bar's five items fit at 320 px.

## Out of scope

- Any change to the destinations, their routes or their pages.
- Toasts shown while the record drawer is open (they sit below the modal drawer today, from 2.27a): a follow-up, not this item.
- Hiding the bar on scroll, or badges on bar items.
