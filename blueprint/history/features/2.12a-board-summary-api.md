## Goal

`GET /api/board/summary` returns the numbers behind the prototype's first
three stat cards, so 2.12b can show them: patients in care and how many
arrived this shift, cases awaiting triage and the oldest wait, and critical
patients and how many have no care team. Door to doctor follows in 2.16a.

## Design reference

`blueprint/reference/vetiflow-er-dashboard.html`, `.stats`:

- **Patients in care** "12", foot "**+3** since 8:00 AM".
- **Awaiting triage** "3", foot "oldest wait **18 min**".
- **Critical patients** "2", foot "Both assigned to a care team".
- **Avg. door to doctor**: not here (2.16a).

## Decisions (the person, 2026-10-28)

- **Shift** (the open decision listed before 2.15a): fixed shifts in the
  clinic's time zone (America/Chicago, confirmed 2026-10-28), from
  configuration until Settings (9.3) owns them. Day
  08:00 to 20:00, Night 20:00 to 08:00. 2.15a reads the same setting.
- **Care team:** a critical case has a care team when a doctor **or** a
  technician is assigned (the card's clinician rule).

## Decisions taken for this item (recommended defaults)

- **Arrived this shift** counts active cases (in care now) whose arrival is
  at or after the current shift's start, so it is a part of "Patients in
  care", as the card reads ("12 … +3 since 8:00 AM"). A case discharged
  during the shift is not counted.
- **Critical** counts every active case with acuity Critical, including an
  incoming case whose provisional priority is Critical (it sits in the
  Critical lane).
- **Oldest wait** is returned as the arrival time of the longest-waiting
  incoming case; the client counts the minutes, as it does on the cards.

## In scope

- **Setting** `Clinic` in `appsettings.json`: `TimeZone` (an IANA name),
  `DayShiftStarts` ("08:00") and `NightShiftStarts` ("20:00"). Read once at
  startup; a missing or unknown value stops startup with a message naming the
  setting (never its connection or secret neighbours).
- **`ClinicShifts`** (`vetiflow.Server/Hosting/`): for a moment in time, the
  current shift (`day` or `night`) and when it began, worked out on the
  clinic's local clock, so a daylight-saving change and a night shift that
  started yesterday both come out right.
- **`GET /api/board/summary`** (`BoardController`, team members only).

## Out of scope

- The stat cards and filters (2.12b); door to doctor (2.16a); the heading's
  shift chip and clinic name (2.15a); editing shifts (9.3).
- Pushing the summary on the hub: 2.12b reloads it with the board on
  `caseUpdated`.

## Data / contracts

- No entity or migration.
- `GET /api/board/summary` → `200`:

  ```json
  {
    "patientsInCare": 12,
    "shift": { "name": "day", "startedAt": "2026-10-28T13:00:00+00:00" },
    "arrivedThisShift": 3,
    "awaitingTriage": 3,
    "oldestAwaitingTriageArrivedAt": "2026-10-28T15:42:00+00:00",
    "criticalPatients": 2,
    "criticalWithoutCareTeam": 0
  }
  ```

  `oldestAwaitingTriageArrivedAt` is null when nothing awaits triage.
  `shift.name` is `day` | `night` (an enum, camelCase text like the others).
  Active means not discharged; awaiting triage means stage Incoming.
- `401` signed out, `403` pet parent, as for `/api/cases`.
- Setting `Clinic:TimeZone`, `Clinic:DayShiftStarts`,
  `Clinic:NightShiftStarts`; later items (2.15a, 2.16a's "last shift", 9.3)
  keep these names.

## Notes for the AI

- .NET on Windows and Linux both accept IANA time zone names
  (`TimeZoneInfo.FindSystemTimeZoneById`).
- The shift start is a local wall-clock time; convert to UTC only after
  choosing the local date, so a DST day doesn't shift it by an hour.
- Keep `CasesController` unchanged; the summary is its own controller.
