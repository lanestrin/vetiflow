## Goal

At tablet and phone widths the patient record opens as a drawer over the board instead of stacking below it, so selecting a patient shows their record at once and closing it returns staff to the same place on the board. Only the drawer scrolls while it is open.

## In scope

- **Breakpoint:** the board and record stay side by side above 1100 px. The record's own 1050 px rules (the 44 px header controls from 3.2f and More actions' toggle) move to 1100 px so they match the drawer. The stats strip's 1050 px rules are unchanged.
- **Drawer mode (1100 px and below):**
  - With no patient selected there is no record panel; the board takes the full width.
  - Selecting a patient opens the record as a modal drawer from the right (a native `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()`, named "Patient record"), over a backdrop. The board, heading and shell behind it are inert while it is open.
  - Focus moves into the drawer when it opens.
  - Three ways to close, all through the board's existing close path (3.2f: the same unsaved-draft guard as choosing another patient, then focus on the patient's name on the board without scrolling): the record's ✕ "Back to patient board", Escape, and a press on the backdrop.
  - If the draft guard keeps the record open ("Keep editing"), the drawer stays open with the draft.
  - Dialogs opened from the record (triage, Update status, tasks, vitals, the review drawers) open above the drawer; Escape closes the top dialog only.
  - Background scrolling is locked while the drawer is open (the page keeps its scroll position); the drawer scrolls on its own and does not chain scrolling to the page.
  - "Loading case…", "This case is no longer on the board." and the load-error state show inside the drawer as they do in the panel today.
- **Phones (700 px and below):** the drawer is full screen with no rounded corners and safe-area padding at the bottom.
- **Crossing the breakpoint while a patient is selected** (for example rotating a tablet): the record moves between the drawer and the side panel and stays selected. It reloads, so it returns to the Overview tab.
- No horizontal page scroll at 1100, 820, 390 and 320 px; drawer controls stay at least 44 px tall.

## Out of scope

- The bottom navigation bar and overflow menu: 2.27b.
- Any change to the record's content, tabs or dialogs.
- Swipe to close.

- `useMediaQuery` returns false without `matchMedia`, so existing tests keep the desktop layout.
