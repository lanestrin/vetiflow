# Feature: Patient record header and tabs

**From build-plan:** feature 3.2c
**Status:** complete
**Branch:** `feature/patient-record-tabs`
**Audit:** not needed - client-only presentation change; no API, auth, data or owner-scope changes.

## Goal

Turn the patient panel into the prototype's patient record: a fixed header that always shows who the patient is, how urgent they are, where they are and their allergy status, above four tabs (Overview, Clinical, Orders & Medications, Timeline). The tabs replace the big record shortcut tiles and pop-up drawers, so staff move between views without leaving the record (product rules section 4; Patient header quick edits decision).

## Design reference

`blueprint/reference/er-board-poc/VetiFlow-ER-Prototype.html`:
- `.patient-sticky`: `.panel-kicker`, `.detail-name-line`, `.detail-meta`, `.quick-fields`, `.allergy-indicator`;
- `.patient-tabs` and `#patient-tabpanel`;
- `.detail-panel` (sticky, scrolls on its own).

`VetiFlow-Product-Rules.md` section 4. The prototype guides hierarchy, not exact pixels. Its inline dropdowns are replaced by buttons that open the 4.3b modals, per the decision.

## In scope

- **Fixed patient header** (replaces the panel's "Patient overview" heading and the hero block):
  - The "Patient record" kicker as the panel's heading, so the panel is named "Patient record". The existing case line ("Case ER-2481 · Checked in …") and the **More actions** menu stay.
  - The species avatar, the patient name (still the focus target after triage), and the acuity badge ("provisional" before triage).
  - The meta line: species · breed · age · weight.
  - Two buttons:
    - **Care stage**, showing the current stage. It opens the existing Update status dialog (4.3b's stage modal).
    - **Location**, showing the room or "No room assigned". It opens the existing Change location dialog.
    - Each has a visible label and an accessible name such as "Change care stage, currently Triage". On a discharged case they are plain text, as the quick edits are today.
  - The allergy line: "Allergies: <summary>" from the existing `allergySummary`. Documented allergies get the existing caution styling, and the line names the status in words.
- **Four tabs** under the header, as a WAI-ARIA tablist:
  - `role="tablist"`, `tab`, `tabpanel`, with `aria-selected`, `aria-controls` and roving `tabindex`.
  - Left and Right arrows move between tabs and wrap; Home and End go to the first and last tab. Focus moves with the selection.
  - A new patient selection always opens on Overview. A board refresh of the same case keeps the chosen tab.
- **Overview tab:** the existing overview sections in their current order, minus what moved to the header:
  - The presenting complaint (read-only).
  - Next care action.
  - Latest vitals.
  - Care team & safety, with **Edit care team**.
  - **View Patient History** (4.21c) as a link at the end.

  The stage and location rows and the "Patient record" tile section leave the Overview. 3.2d reorders this tab.
- **Clinical tab:** the content the Clinical drawer shows today (latest recorded vitals with measured and entered times, observations, the change summary, allergy detail), unchanged.
- **Orders & Medications tab:** the content the Medications drawer shows today (intake and patient-record medication text with its reconciliation status), plus the line "No orders are recorded in VetiFlow yet." No order or administration data is invented.
- **Timeline tab:** the content the Timeline drawer shows today, unchanged.
- **Reuse, not duplication.** Each review's content becomes its own component: `ClinicalReview`, `MedicationReview`, `TimelineReview`.
  - The Clinical and Timeline drawers become thin wrappers around that content, because the visit record page (4.21d) still opens them as drawers.
  - The Medications drawer, which only the panel used, is replaced by its content component.
  - The panel no longer opens any drawer.
- **Desktop layout:** beside the board (above the current 1050 px stacking width), the panel stays in view while the board scrolls. It scrolls on its own, with the header fixed at its top. When stacked under the board, the panel scrolls with the page and the header is not fixed. The tablet drawer and full-screen phone panel are 2.27.
- **States kept as they are:** no selection, loading, "no longer on the board", the load failure with **Try again**, and dialogs that never carry over to the next selection.

## Out of scope

- Tablet drawer, full-screen phone panel and bottom navigation (2.27).
- The demo role switch (2.28).
- The visit record page's own layout (it keeps its Clinical and Timeline drawers).
- Any API or data change.
