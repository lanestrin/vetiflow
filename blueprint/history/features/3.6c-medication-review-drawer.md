# Feature: Medication review drawer

**From build-plan:** feature 3.6c
**Status:** complete
**Branch:** `feature/medication-review-drawer`
**Audit:** recommended - medication-source accuracy and patient-bound modal lifecycle.

## Goal

Review the selected encounter's recorded medication text from Patient Overview,
with honest source labels and unknown reconciliation status.

## Design reference

- `blueprint/reference/patient-overview/VETIFLOW_ER_OVERVIEW_SPECIFICATION.md`,
  section 6, and the user's updated `VetiFlow_ER_Overview_Next_Care_Action.html`.
- Match the HTML's final Patient record treatment: Clinical / Medications /
  Timeline as three equal white outlined tiles, icons above labels, before
  vitals. Preserve the application's accessible focus token and readable text.
  The broader overview restructuring remains 2.23.
- Preserve the user's reference replacement and specification edits. The POC's
  fictional medication records never become application data.

## In scope

- Reuse `CaseReviewDrawer`, titled Medications with patient/case identity, Close,
  Escape, focus return and one open encounter-bound view.
- Show **Recorded at intake** from `detail.medicationsAtIntake` and **Patient
  record** from `detail.patient.currentMedications`, independently. Patient
  history is not proof of current use at this encounter.
- Show **Reconciliation status: Unknown (verification not recorded)**. Null,
  empty and whitespace-only sources show **Not recorded; medication use
  unknown**. Preserve literal text/line breaks, including explicit negatives;
  never merge, backfill, deduplicate or infer verified medication facts.
- Reuse loaded-only access, same-case refresh without resetting focus, and
  closing on deselection, patient change, missing/failed/denied detail. Late
  responses and delayed close events must not affect another encounter/view.

## Out of scope

Prescribing, editing, reconciliation actions, orders, administrations, invented
doses/times/actors or parsed medication fields. Structured history and authority
remain 4.18/4.19; role/field filtering remains S.6/3.7.
