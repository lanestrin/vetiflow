## Goal

The Team page shows who is on the team and who is on the floor, and what each
clinician is working on: their status and their active cases, derived from case data.
It replaces the Team placeholder in the rail.

## Design reference

None in `blueprint/reference/`. Agreed by the person on 2026-11-01: a simple grouped
list in the existing design tokens, like the other staff pages. It is not a recreation
of a design, so no image is needed.

## Approved policy

Answers from the person on 2026-11-01:

- **Who shows:** every active veterinarian and technician, in two groups: **On the floor**
  first (not Unavailable), then **Unavailable**. Unavailable clinicians are shown, so the
  page also tells you who is not around.
- **Cases:** each clinician's active-case count, then each case's number and patient
  name. Clinical text stays team-only, as on the board.
- **Layout:** a grouped list, one row per clinician: name, role, status, active case
  count, and the case list.

Rules carried over from earlier items:

- "On the floor" and "active case" mean what they mean on the on-floor list (2.21a):
  not Unavailable, and cases that are not discharged and have the clinician as assigned
  doctor or technician. A case counts once per clinician.
- Only veterinarians and technicians have a status and appear here. Assistants,
  receptionists and managers do not (2.21a).
- A deactivated staff member does not appear.

## In scope

- `GET /api/staff/team`: the clinicians above, with their status and active cases.
- The Team route in the client, replacing the Coming Soon placeholder.
- A grouped list with a heading for each group, a row per clinician, and each case as a
  row beneath the clinician's row.
- Loading, empty, error and session states, as the board uses them.
- A reload when a case changes (`caseUpdated`), as the board does.

## Out of scope

- Editing a clinician's status here (My status stays in the top bar, 2.21b).
- Assigning cases from this page (4.3).
- Links from a case to the board. Case rows are text.
- Assistants, receptionists and managers on the team list (2.21a).
- Reports, Overview and Settings (2.18, 2.19, 9.3).
- Live updates for status changes: a status change shows on the next load, because status
  changes are not broadcast today. Recorded here so it is a stated limit, not a surprise.

## Data / contracts

- **New route:** `GET /api/staff/team`, team-member policy. Response: a list of
  `{ id, displayName, initials, role, status, activeCaseCount, activeCases: [{ id, caseNumber, patientName }] }`,
  with the On the floor group first, then the Unavailable group, each sorted by display
  name. `activeCaseCount` equals the length of `activeCases`.
- **Data:** no migration, no new column. Reads `StaffMembers`, `Accounts` for the signed-in
  check, and `EmergencyCases` with their doctor and technician.
- **Keep for later:** the group rule (On the floor = not Unavailable) and the active-case rule
  are the same as 2.21a. Any later change to one must change both.

## Security

- Team members only: `TeamMemberPolicy`. Owners get 403, as the other staff endpoints.
- The response carries names, roles, statuses and patient names only: no contact
  details, no owner details, no clinical notes.
- Never log the response or the patient names.
