## Goal

Staff can see at a glance how long patients are waiting for a veterinarian this shift, compared with last shift, and can jump to the patients still waiting. The numbers come from 2.16a's `doorToDoctor` in the board summary.

## Decisions (2026-11-03, the person)

- **Placement:** a chip in the board heading, beside the shift indicator: **Door to doctor · 14 min ↓4**. Pressing it opens the **Door to first doctor** dialog with the previous-shift comparison and **Show waiting patients**.
- The four-stat strip stays as it is (2.26a), focused on the current caseload, and keeps its compact phone layout.
- If the heading gets tight on phones, the chip wraps onto the next line.

## Design reference

The prototype (`blueprint/reference/er-board-poc/VetiFlow-ER-Prototype.html`, `header.png`) has no door-to-doctor element, so there's nothing to match element by element. The chip follows the heading's existing look: small muted text like the shift indicator (`.shift`), as a pill button. The dialog follows the app's existing native `<dialog>` pattern (`DemoNoticeDialog`).

## In scope

- **The chip** (`BoardHeading`), shown when the summary loaded, as a button:
  - The text is "Door to doctor · 14 min", using `doorToDoctor.currentShift.averageMinutes`. It shows "Door to doctor · —" when nobody has been assessed this shift.
  - **The trend** appears when both shifts have an average: `current − previous` minutes.
    - Negative shows "↓4" (faster than last shift); positive shows "↑3" (slower).
    - Equal shows "=" (same as last shift).
    - With no trend, nothing follows the minutes.
    - The arrow and number are never shown by colour alone. The button's accessible name spells it out, for example "Door to doctor, 14 minutes this shift, 4 minutes faster than last shift", or "Door to doctor, no assessments yet this shift".
  - At phone width (≤ 700 px) the heading's right-hand row wraps, so the chip goes onto its own line rather than squeezing New intake. The chip is at least 44 px tall there.
- **The dialog** (`DoorToDoctorDialog`, a native modal `<dialog>`), titled "Door to first doctor":
  - **This shift:** "14 min average · 4 patients assessed". With none assessed: "No vet assessments recorded yet".
  - **Last shift:** the same wording, from `previousShift`.
  - **The trend line**, when both shifts have an average: "4 min faster than last shift", "3 min slower than last shift" or "Same as last shift".
  - **Waiting:** "3 patients waiting for a vet assessment" (1 patient / 0 patients).
  - A note on how it's measured: "Arrival to the recorded vet assessment, for patients who arrived during each shift."
  - **Buttons:**
    - **Show waiting patients:** closes the dialog and filters the board to waiting patients. With none waiting it stays enabled and, as the stats do, shows the toast "No patients waiting for a vet" and leaves the board as it is.
    - **Close.** Escape and a backdrop click also close it, and focus returns to the chip.
  - The numbers come from the summary the board already holds. A live update (`caseUpdated`) refreshes the summary, so an open dialog updates too.
- **The board filter** `awaitingVet`:
  - It's added to `BoardFilter`. It shows active cases with no recorded vet assessment (`vetAssessment === null`), the same patients the summary counts in `waiting`.
  - The footer note reads " · waiting for a vet assessment".
  - No stat in the strip is pressed while it's on. Pressing **In care** clears it, as it does any other stat filter.
- **Build plan:** record the placement decision in Decisions (replacing the open "Door to doctor placement"), and reword 2.16b's line to the heading chip.

## Out of scope

- Recording the assessment, Assign next action, and the board and record milestone labels: 2.16c.
- Any API change: the API is 2.16a's, unchanged.
- A door-to-doctor stat in the strip: not wanted.
- Per-patient waiting times in the dialog (names, minutes): Show waiting patients uses the board instead.
