## Goal

The patient record's Clinical tab looks like the prototype's: the veterinarian's examination and treatment plan workspace, then signed documentation, vet review requests, diagnostics, handoff and discharge. Controls whose back end does not exist yet are shown in place but disabled, each saying which build item enables it, so staff see where the work will happen without anything pretending to save. The detail the tab shows today (recorded vitals, change since the previous set, mentation, allergy source and verification) stays available in two collapsed sections. The header's allergy indicator opens the allergy details.

## In scope

- **Order of the tab:**
  1. Examination & treatment plan
  2. Signed documentation & nursing notes
  3. Vet review requests
  4. Diagnostics & follow-up
  5. Handoff & escalation
  6. Discharge
  7. Recorded vitals & assessment
  8. Allergy details
  9. The save-state line
- **Unavailable controls:**
  - Buttons use `aria-disabled="true"`, not `disabled`, so keyboard and screen-reader users can still reach them and hear why. A click does nothing.
  - Each has a `title` tooltip and the same text through `aria-describedby`, for example: "Available when vet review requests are built (4.16b)."
  - The three form fields are `disabled`, with the visible hint below them giving the reason. The hint also carries the field's description.
  - Tooltip texts:

    | Control | Tooltip |
    | --- | --- |
    | Fields and **Save draft** | "Available when clinical drafts are saved in VetiFlow (4.15b)." |
    | **Sign off** | "Available when clinical note signing is built (4.15d)." |
    | **＋ Request** | "Available when vet review requests are built (4.16b)." |
    | **Track diagnostic** | "Available when diagnostics are tracked (4.20b)." |
    | **Record handoff / concern** | "Available when handoffs are recorded (6.1b)." |
    | **Review discharge readiness** | "Available when discharge authorization is built (6.2b)." |

- **Disclosures:**
  - They are native `<details>`/`<summary>`, closed by default, with a `+` / `−` marker and the summary value on the right.
  - Summary values and empty states:

    | Disclosure | Summary value | Content |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Signed documentation & nursing notes | "0" | "No signed documentation or nursing notes recorded." |
    | Diagnostics & follow-up | "Not tracked yet" | Track diagnostic, then "Diagnostics aren't tracked in VetiFlow yet. Diagnostic tasks and blockers are in Tasks and due items." |
    | Handoff & escalation | "Not tracked yet" | Record handoff / concern, then "Handoffs aren't recorded in VetiFlow yet. Urgent concerns require direct communication." |
    | Discharge | "Not recorded", or "Discharged <time>" | "Medical authorization is separate from preparation, checkout and physical departure.", then Review discharge readiness |

  - Vet review requests is a section, not a disclosure, as in the PNG. Its empty state is "No review requests recorded."
- **Recorded vitals & assessment:** a disclosure whose summary is the latest set's measured time, or "None recorded". Inside are today's latest recorded vitals (with measured time, entry time and recorder), "Since the previous saved set" and the clinical assessment findings, unchanged.
- **Allergy details:**
  - A disclosure whose summary is the allergy status label. Inside is today's allergy record, unchanged: status, reported allergies, source, verifier and time, and this visit's intake.
  - The header's "Allergies: …" line becomes a button. Its visible text and `data-status` are unchanged, and its accessible name is "Allergies: <summary>. Show allergy details".
  - Pressing it selects the Clinical tab, opens Allergy details, scrolls it into view and moves focus to its summary. It works on a discharged case too, because it is read-only.
- **Visit record drawer:**
  - The visit record page's Clinical drawer (4.21d) keeps today's content.
  - `ClinicalReview` is split into `RecordedVitalsAndAssessment` and `AllergyRecord`. The drawer composes both, and the tab puts them in the two disclosures.
- **Phones:**
  - At 480 px and below the buttons are at least 44 px tall and wrap; the textareas use 16 px text.
  - No horizontal scroll at 390 px and 320 px.

## Out of scope

- Saving drafts, signing, notes, review requests, diagnostics, handoffs or discharge authorization: 4.15a-d, 4.16a/b, 4.20a/b, 6.1a/b, 6.2a/b. Nothing is kept in browser storage.
- The technician variant of the tab: 2.28.
- The desktop close ✕ and the other record-panel gaps found in the PNG review: they wait for the person's decision.
- Overview, Orders & Medications and Timeline: unchanged.
