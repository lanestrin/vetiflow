## Goal

The ER board page gets the prototype's heading: the clinic's name as an
eyebrow, the "ER Command Center" title and subtitle, a live shift chip ("Day
shift · 4 clinicians on floor"), and the **New intake** button that already
opens the intake dialog (4.1b). It replaces today's plain "VetiFlow ER Board"
title.

## Design reference

`blueprint/reference/vetiflow-er-dashboard.html`, `.heading`:

```
<div class="heading">
  <div>
    <div class="eyebrow">Northside Veterinary Emergency</div>
    <h1>ER Command Center</h1>
    <div class="sub">Live patient flow and care coordination</div>
  </div>
  <div class="headright">
    <div class="shift"><i class="live"></i> Day shift <b>·</b> 4 clinicians on floor</div>
    <button class="btn" id="intake">＋ <span>New intake</span></button>
  </div>
</div>
```

- **`.heading`:** flex, `align-items: flex-end`, space-between, margin-bottom
  22px.
- **`.eyebrow`:** teal, 11px, uppercase, letter-spacing 1.2px, 700, margin-bottom
  7px.
- **`h1`:** 800 27px, letter-spacing -0.7px. Manrope arrives with 2.20.
- **`.sub`:** muted, margin-top 6px.
- **`.headright`:** flex, gap 10px.
- **`.shift`:** flex, gap 8px, `#61737a` 12px, padding 9px 12px, `--line`
  border, radius 10px, white.
- **`.live`:** an 8px dot, `#20a88d`, with a 3px `#dff5ee` ring.
- **`.btn`:** the existing `.intakeButton`.
- **At 650px:** the heading stacks (column, gap 14px), the `h1` is 23px, the
  `.sub` margin-top is 4px, and `.headright` takes the full width with
  space-between and the button `flex: 1`.

## In scope

- **`BoardHeading`** takes the board summary (or null) and an `onIntake`
  callback. `ErBoardPage` shows it in place of its current header. The
  intake button keeps its current behaviour.
- **From the summary (2.12a, 2.15a):**
  - the eyebrow is `clinicName`;
  - the chip reads "Day shift" or "Night shift" from `shift.name`, then " · ",
    then the clinician count: "1 clinician on floor", or "N clinicians on
    floor" for any other number, including 0.
  - The chip and eyebrow update whenever the summary reloads (every board
    update).
- **Summary missing:** while the summary is unavailable (`null`, as the stat
  cards already handle), the eyebrow and the chip are left out. The title,
  subtitle and button always show.
- **The client's `BoardSummary` type and `buildBoardSummary`** gain
  `clinicName` and `cliniciansOnFloor`.
- **Accessibility:**
  - one `h1`, "ER Command Center";
  - the dot is decorative (`aria-hidden`), and the chip's text carries the
    meaning;
  - the eyebrow is a paragraph above the `h1`, not a heading.

## Out of scope

- The prototype's fonts (2.20). Live updates when someone else changes their
  status (no status hub message yet; the count follows the next reload).
- Changing the shift on the clock at 08:00 or 20:00 without a reload.
- The breadcrumb, which already says "ER Command Center" (2.10b).
