## Goal

In the patient overview panel, staff tick a task done (and untick it to
reopen it), and see when the next reassessment and client update are due. An
overdue one stands out in words and colour, so nobody misses a recheck.
Adding tasks and setting due times follow in 4.4c.

## Design reference

`blueprint/reference/vetiflow-er-dashboard.html`, the panel's rows under
Location:

```
<div class="task"><span>⏱</span><span class="tasklabel">Next reassessment</span><span class="taskmeta">Due in 8 min</span></div>
<div class="task"><span>☎</span><span class="tasklabel">Client update</span><span class="taskmeta">Due 11:15 AM</span></div>
```

And the Next actions checklist:

```
<div class="task"><span class="check done">✓</span><span class="tasklabel">Place IV catheter</span><span class="taskmeta">Dr. Reed</span></div>
<div class="task"><span class="check"></span><span class="tasklabel">Review radiographs</span><span class="taskmeta">Pending</span></div>
```

- The rows reuse the panel's existing `.row`, `.rowLabel` and `.rowMeta`
  (3.2b), and the checklist already has `.check`.
- With no due item, a row shows "Not scheduled", as the prototype's
  `info.recheck || 'Not scheduled'` does. The prototype says "Not recorded"
  for Client update; "Not scheduled" fits a due time better and is used for
  both.
- **Overdue:** the time turns `--color-acuity-critical` (the prototype's
  `.alert`, `--red`) and reads "Overdue by 12 min", so it doesn't rely on
  colour alone.

## In scope

- **The due rows:** "⏱ Next reassessment" and "☎ Client update", under
  Location, from the case detail's `nextReassessment` and `nextClientUpdate`
  (4.4a). The time comes from `dueLabel(dueAt, now)` in `overviewFormat.ts`:
  - **Overdue** (`dueAt` before now): "Overdue by 12 min", then "Overdue by
    1 h 5 min" from an hour.
  - **Within the next hour:** "Due in 8 min", or "Due now" within the minute.
  - **Later:** "Due 11:15 AM", using `timeLabel`, so another day shows its
    date.
  - **None:** "Not scheduled".

  The panel's clock (`useNow`) moves them on without a reload.
- **Ticks:** each checklist task's `.check` becomes a native checkbox,
  labelled by the task's description and styled as today.
  - Ticking calls `POST .../tasks/{taskId}/complete`, and unticking calls
    `.../reopen`.
  - The tick changes at once and is disabled while the request is in flight.
  - On success, the panel and board reload, as after triage. On any failure
    other than an ended session, the tick goes back, a toast says "Couldn't
    update the task. Try again.", and the panel reloads, so a task another
    person changed or a case just discharged shows its real state.
  - An ended session goes to sign-in (`useLeaveWhenRefused`).
- **The checklist's meta:**
  - a done task shows who completed it (`resolvedBy`), else "Done";
  - an open task shows its assignee, else "Pending".
- **Client types:**
  - `CaseTaskType` gains `reassessment` and `clientUpdate`;
  - `CaseTask` gains `createdBy` and `resolvedBy`;
  - `CaseDetail` gains `nextReassessment` and `nextClientUpdate` (a new
    `CaseDueItem` type);
  - the test builders follow.

  The checklist keeps showing only `task` items, and blockers stay in the
  timeline.

## Defaults chosen with this spec (listed for review)

- **"Due in N min" up to 60 minutes, then the clock time.** The prototype
  shows both forms without a rule.
- **"Not scheduled"** for both empty rows.
- **No success toast for a tick**, as the prototype's ticks have none. Only
  failures toast.

## Out of scope

- **＋ Add task**, and setting or changing a due time (4.4c). The link stays
  disabled.
- Ticking blockers or due items off from the panel. They are resolved from
  their own rows in 4.4c and later.
- The board card's tags, which already show open blockers (2.11b).
