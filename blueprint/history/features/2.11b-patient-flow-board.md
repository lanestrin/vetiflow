## Goal

The ER board shows the prototype's **Patient flow**: three acuity lanes with
the new cards, instead of the six stage lanes.

## Design reference

`blueprint/reference/vetiflow-er-dashboard.html`: `.board`, `.boardtop`
("Patient flow", live dot, "Updated just now · N active cases"), `.lanes` and
`.lane.critical/.urgent/.stable` with `.lanehead` counts, `.patient` cards
(`.petname`, `.species`, `.wait`, `.owner`, `.tags`, `.casefoot`,
`.miniavatar`), `.patient.selected`, `.new-card` incoming cards, `.flowfoot`,
and at 650 px `.mobiletabs` showing one lane at a time.

## In scope

- **Lanes:** Critical, Urgent, Stable with counts, cards in the API's order. A
  "Not triaged" lane appears only when a case has no acuity (old data only).
- **Card:** name; "Dog · Golden Retriever · 8y" (age from the date of birth:
  years, or months under one year); the wait chip (STAT, Ready, Recheck, New,
  or minutes since arrival); "Owner · complaint" (complaint alone without an
  owner); tags; "<stage> · N min" (minutes in stage), or "Awaiting triage" for
  an incoming case; the clinician's initials and short name, or "--
  Unassigned".
- **Tags:** an incoming case shows "<Priority> · provisional" in its acuity's
  tone; open blockers show as alert tags. Clinical tags come with 3.5.
- **Selecting** a card (click or keyboard) marks it selected (`aria-pressed`);
  the overview panel that uses it is 3.2b.
- **Board top and footer:** "Updated just now" (or "Updated N min ago") ·
  "N active cases"; footer "Showing N of N active cases".
- **Live:** minutes and the "updated" time tick every 30 s; the hub reload
  stays as it is.
- **650 px:** lane tabs (Critical, Urgent, Stable) show one lane at a time.
- The client type gains the 2.11a fields. The old stage lanes and cards
  (`WorkflowLane`, `CaseCard`, `CaseStatusLabel`, `CaseTimer`) are removed;
  `AcuityBadge` stays for the panel (3.2b).

## Out of scope

- Stat cards (2.12b), heading (2.15b), Filter, Priority, Incoming and search
  (2.14), the overview panel (3.2b), clinical tags (3.5).

## Data / contracts

No API change. The client type adds `caseNumber`, `patientDateOfBirth`,
`ownerName`, `stageEnteredAt`, `waitChip`, `clinician { id, shortName,
initials }` and `room.type`, as 2.11a returns them.

Prototype text colours below AA are darkened just enough (as for the shell and
acuity): muted text uses `--color-shell-muted`; new tokens for the wait chip,
the info tag, the mini avatar and the lane count.
