## Goal

A clinician completes triage on an incoming case: confirms or changes the
provisional priority from intake, and the case leaves Incoming, so the board
stops showing **New** and "Awaiting triage". Later the priority can be changed
again (re-triage). Every change goes through `RecordTriage` and adds a
`Triaged` entry to the timeline.

## Design reference

`blueprint/reference/vetiflow-er-dashboard.html`, `#triageAction` ("✓ Complete
triage", shown only for incoming cases): the card's chip changes from New to
STAT or the priority, its foot reads "Triage complete · awaiting assignment",
the triage count drops by one, and the timeline gets "Triage completed".

## Decisions taken for this item (recommended defaults, 2026-10-28)

- **Stage after triage:** Incoming moves to **Triage** (the existing stage
  between Incoming and Treatment: triaged, waiting for the care team). The
  stage clock restarts. Re-triage of a case past Incoming keeps its stage.
- **Who:** any team member (`TeamMemberPolicy`) until staff roles exist; the
  build plan already says "clinicians only once roles exist".
- **Discharged cases** cannot be triaged: 409.
- **Timeline wording:** first triage "Triage completed · Urgent"; re-triage
  "Re-triaged · Urgent → Critical", or "Re-triaged · Urgent confirmed" when the
  priority is unchanged. No triage notes in the description (notes stay on the
  assessment, team only).

## In scope

- `POST /api/cases/{id}/triage` with `{ "priority": "urgent", "notes": "…" }`.
- A new `TriageAssessment` through `RecordTriage`, by the signed-in staff
  member, with the notes.
- Incoming → Triage; a `Triaged` `CaseEvent`; `caseUpdated` on the hub.

## Out of scope

- The panel's **Complete triage** button, re-triage menu and the card update
  (4.2b).
- Role checks (clinicians only) until roles exist.
- Pain score at triage (its own item).

## Data / contracts

- **Request:** `priority` (required, `critical` | `urgent` | `stable`),
  `notes` (optional, up to 2000, cleaned like intake text).
- **Response:** 204. The board and panel reload on `caseUpdated`.
- **Errors:** 400 (missing or unknown priority, notes too long), 401, 403
  (pet parent), 404 (unknown case), 409 (discharged).
- No schema change: `TriageAssessment`, `CaseEvent` and `CaseStage.Triage`
  already exist.

## Security

- Team members only. Owners never reach it.
- Notes are clinical text: never logged, never in the event description or the
  hub message (the hub sends only the case id).
