## Goal

The four stat cards from the prototype sit above the board, filled from
`GET /api/board/summary` (2.12a) and reloaded whenever the board reloads.
Three of them filter the board, so staff can see the triage queue or the
critical patients with one click.

## Design reference

`blueprint/reference/vetiflow-er-dashboard.html`, `.stats` and its scripts
(`showAllCases`, `setTriageFilter`, `setCriticalFilter`, `activeKpi`):

- A grid of four white cards (`.stat`): border `--line`, radius 14px,
  padding 16px 18px. The head row (`.stathead`) has a 12px/600 muted label and
  an icon on the right. The number (`.statnum`) is 800 25px. The foot
  (`.statfoot`) is 11px `#819096`, with `strong` in teal.
- **Patients in care** "12", icon "↗", foot "**+3** since 8:00 AM".
- **Awaiting triage** "3", a dot icon, foot "Click to view incoming cases ·
  oldest wait **18 min**" (the wait in amber).
- **Critical patients** "2", a red "♥", foot "Both assigned to a care team".
- **Avg. door to doctor**, icon "◷": "—" here, until 2.16b.
- A clickable card has a hover ring. A pressed card has a teal border, a mint
  ring and an "ACTIVE" pill (`.active-state`) in the head. Patients in care is
  pressed when the page opens.
- At 1050px there are 2 columns. At 650px the gap is 8px, the padding 12px
  and the number 21px.

## In scope

- **`BoardStats`** (`components/board/`) replaces `ErBoardStatusBar` (its
  component, styles and test are removed). The three clickable cards are
  buttons with `aria-pressed`. Door to doctor is a plain card showing "—",
  and is not a button until 2.16b.
- **Card text:**
  - Patients in care: `patientsInCare`; foot "**+N** since <shift start>".
    The shift start is shown in the browser's local time ("8:00 AM"); clinic
    workstations are in the clinic's zone.
  - Awaiting triage: `awaitingTriage`; foot "Click to view incoming cases ·
    oldest wait **N min**". The minutes are counted from
    `oldestAwaitingTriageArrivedAt` with the board's ticking clock
    (`useNow`, `minutesSince`). With nobody waiting, the foot reads "No
    patients awaiting triage".
  - Critical patients: `criticalPatients`. The foot depends on the count:
    - none critical: "No critical patients";
    - all covered: "Assigned to a care team" for 1, "Both assigned to a care
      team" for 2, "All assigned to a care team" for 3 or more;
    - otherwise: "**N** without a care team", in red.
- **Board filter** (`all`, `awaitingTriage` or `critical`), held by
  `ErBoardPage` and passed to `PatientFlowBoard`:
  - `all`: every lane and card. The foot reads "Showing N of N active cases",
    as today.
  - `awaitingTriage`: only cards in stage Incoming, in their lanes. At phone
    width every lane is stacked rather than tabbed, as in the prototype. The
    foot reads "Showing N awaiting triage cases · initial priority is
    provisional".
  - `critical`: only the Critical lane, and the phone tab switches to it. The
    foot reads "Showing N critical patients · other acuity lanes hidden".
  - Pressing Awaiting triage when its count is 0 keeps the current filter and
    shows the toast "No patients awaiting triage", as in the prototype.
    Pressing the pressed card again changes nothing. Patients in care is the
    way back to `all`.
- **Loading:** `loadCases` also fetches the summary (`getBoardSummary` in a new
  `services/boardApi.ts`), so the cards reload on every `caseUpdated`, after
  intake and after triage.
  - If the summary request fails but the cases load, the board still shows.
    The card numbers show "—" and the feet read "Not available".
  - A `401` or `403` from either request is handled as the board handles it
    now.

## Out of scope

- Door to doctor's value, its details dialog and **Show waiting patients**
  (2.16b).
- The top bar search, the **Incoming** toggle, **Filter** and **Priority**
  (2.14).
- The heading's eyebrow, title and shift chip (2.15b). The current title stays.
- Manrope and DM Sans (2.20). The cards use the current font stack.
- Refreshing the counts at the shift change when no case changes. The next
  board reload picks the new shift up.

## Data / contracts

- No server change. The client reads `GET /api/board/summary` exactly as 2.12a
  returns it:
  `{ patientsInCare, shift: { name, startedAt }, arrivedThisShift,
  awaitingTriage, oldestAwaitingTriageArrivedAt | null, criticalPatients,
  criticalWithoutCareTeam }`.
- `BoardFilter = "all" | "awaitingTriage" | "critical"`. 2.16b adds
  `"waitingForDoctor"` and 2.14 builds on the same filter.

## Security

- No new surface. The summary endpoint is team-members only. A pet parent's
  `403` sends them to `/pet-parent`, as the board does today.
- The cards show counts and times only. No owner details are shown.

## Notes for the AI

- The prototype is the design: copy its `.stat*` values into the SCSS
  module, using the existing tokens where they match (`--color-shell-line`,
  `--color-shell-muted`, `--color-care-teal`, `--color-care-mint`).
- Keep the filtering in `PatientFlowBoard`. Pass it the filter, not a
  pre-filtered list, so the foot can name what is hidden.
- Load the cases and the summary in one `Promise.allSettled`. The board's
  existing error handling stays for the cases request.
- Keep the stat number as the server's count. Don't recount it from the
  loaded cases.
