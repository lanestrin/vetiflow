# Feature: Show the allergy status truthfully

**From build-plan:** feature 4.12b
**Status:** complete
**Branch:** `feature/allergy-status-summary`
**Audit:** recommended - clinical safety wording on the ER board; worth checking that no state reads as a verified negative unless it is one.

## Goal

Since 4.12a each patient and visit stores an explicit allergy status, but the
screens still guess from free text: the Patient Overview merges the patient's
and the visit's text and shows "Unknown / not verified" even when nothing was
recorded. Staff now see the stored status: not recorded, unknown or not
verified (with what was reported), no known allergies (verified), or documented
allergies (verified), with who verified it and when in the Clinical drawer.

## In scope

- A pure helper `allergySummary(detail)` in
  `components/overview/allergyStatus.ts` returning the wording for the patient's
  status, from `patient.allergyStatus` and `allergyStatusAtIntake` only (never
  from the text's content):
  - `notRecorded`: "Not recorded";
  - `unknownOrUnverified`: "<text> · not verified", or "Unknown · not verified"
    with no text;
  - `noKnownAllergies`: "No known allergies · verified";
  - `documentedAllergies`: "<text> · verified";
  - when this visit's intake recorded nothing (`allergyStatusAtIntake` is
    `notRecorded`) and the patient has a record, it adds "not rechecked at this
    visit's intake".
- **Patient Overview** "Care team & safety", Allergies row: shows the summary
  instead of today's merged text. The intake's own text is no longer merged in;
  since 4.12a the intake updates the patient's record, so it is already shown.
- **Clinical drawer:** a new "Allergies" section, after "Clinical assessment",
  as a description list:
  - Status (the four labels above, without the text);
  - Reported allergies: the text, or "None recorded";
  - Source: "Owner reported", "Referring clinic records", "This clinic's record",
    or "Not recorded";
  - Verified: "<name> · <date and time>", or "Not verified";
  - At this visit's intake: the intake text with "not verified", or "Not
    recorded at this visit's intake".

## Out of scope

- Any way to verify or edit allergies, and who may (S.6 and the clinical
  workflow); nothing in the app can set a verified status yet, so only "not
  recorded" and "unknown or not verified" appear on real data today.
- The intake form (unchanged); medications; the visit record page's own
  summary (it gains the section only through the shared Clinical drawer).
- Server or data changes.
