## Goal

The Patient Overview's Next care action block shows the task staff designated as the Next Care Action (4.13a), with the priority staff gave it, who is responsible and whether it is due or overdue, so the next step is clear at a glance. When nothing is designated it says so plainly, and keeps any older free-text note visible as what it is.

## Design reference

- **The current block** (3.2d, matching the prototype's `.action` "NEXT CARE ACTION" block and `blueprint/reference/er-board-poc/Overview.png`): kicker with the warning icon, a title line, then rows (Responsible, Next reassessment, Last owner update, Next client update). This item changes what the title and the first rows say; the block's look is unchanged.
- **Prototype script** (`VetiFlow-ER-Prototype.html`, `overview`): the title is the next open task's title, then "owner · Due …"; with no open task, "No outstanding care tasks". The app does not copy the prototype's choice of task: it shows only the staff-designated one (decisions below).
- Decisions in `blueprint/build-plan.md` and 4.13a's history: clinical priority and the Next Care Action designation are explicit staff decisions; the software never selects, ranks or promotes a task; null priority means "Not prioritized"; the legacy `nextAction` text is never matched to a task; due-time labels are informational only.
- Product rules section 4 (Overview priority 2: "Next care action, responsible person and due time") and section 8 ("Task completion does not mean the patient is stable…").

## In scope

- **The designated task** is `detail.tasks` entry whose `id` is `detail.nextActionTaskId`. No other task is ever shown in its place: an open task that is not designated, the task due soonest and the highest-priority task are all ignored.
- **With a designated task**, the block shows:
  - **Title:** the task's description.
  - **Priority** row: "Urgent", "High" or "Routine" as staff set it, or "Not prioritized".
  - **Responsible** row: the assignee's name, or "Not assigned".
  - **Due** row: the existing `dueLabel` text ("Overdue by 10 min", "Due now", "Due in 5 min", "Due 14:30", "Not scheduled"), updating with the panel's clock. When overdue, the row adds a warning icon and the warning text colour; the words "Overdue by …" carry the meaning, not the colour alone.
- **With no designated task** (none set, or `nextActionTaskId` names a task the detail no longer lists):
  - **Title:** "No next action designated".
  - If the legacy `nextAction` text is not blank, beneath it: "Previously documented next-action note:" and the text, styled as secondary text so it does not read as the current action.
  - No Priority, Responsible or Due rows.
- **Unchanged:** the Next reassessment, Last owner update and Next client update rows; the block's heading and look; designating, prioritizing and clearing in Tasks and due items (4.4a/4.13a), which reload the panel as today. Completing the designated task leaves the block on "No next action designated" (4.13a clears the designation); it never says the action is done or resolved.
- Discharged cases show the same block read-only (4.13a clears their designation on discharge).

## Out of scope

- Setting priority or designating from the overview itself: Tasks and due items already does both.
- Assigner tracking and reassignment history: 4.13e.
- Vet review requests: 4.16a/c.
- Any server, data or API change.

- Reuse `dueLabel` and the panel's `useNow` clock; add no new time formatting.
- Keep the block's existing rows and order after the new ones.
