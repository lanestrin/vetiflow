## Goal

The ER board's heading and toolbar match the designer's revision: a compact heading with the shift status and New intake beside it, an All patients / My patients scope on the board, and a toolbar with its own search box, an Unassigned toggle, Filter and a clearly labelled Time in stage ↓ sort. Staff find unassigned patients in one click, and the sort says what it does.

## In scope

- **Page heading** (`BoardHeading`):
  - The eyebrow (when the summary has loaded) and the title stay; the subtitle goes.
  - On the right: the shift status as plain text with its live dot, then **New intake**.
  - Two columns on desktop; at 700 px and below the heading stacks and the shift status and New intake share one row, as in the prototype.
- **Scope switch** (replaces `BoardViewSwitch` and its hint line, which are deleted):
  - Beside "Patient board" in the board's header: a group named "Patient scope" with two buttons.
  - **All patients** shows the number of cases on the board and is selected (`aria-pressed="true"`).
  - **My patients** uses `aria-disabled="true"`, stays focusable, does nothing on click, and has the tooltip and description "Available when the demo role switch is built (2.28)." (the 3.2e pattern).
- **Board search box** in the toolbar:
  - A search input named "Search the board", placeholder "Search patients...".
  - It and the top bar's "Search patients" share one value: typing in either updates both and filters the board with today's `matchesSearch`.
  - Enter in the board box does nothing more (no navigation); Enter in the top bar still opens Patients search with the query, as today.
  - The footer's "Search all patients" link (4.6a) is unchanged.
- **Unassigned** toggle:
  - A toggle button (`aria-pressed`) that shows only cases missing a primary technician or an attending veterinarian, or both.
  - It combines with the stat filter, the Filter menu and search (all must match). The footer count reflects it.
- **Sort**: 2.14d's button keeps its behaviour (longest time in stage first within the fixed Critical, Urgent, Stable order). Its visible label becomes "Time in stage ↓", its accessible name "Time in stage", with `aria-pressed` and the tooltip and description "Longest time in current stage first, within each acuity. Acuity order stays Critical, Urgent, Stable."
- **Incoming toggle** removed. The Awaiting triage stat keeps filtering to incoming cases (unchanged).
- **Phones (700 px and below):** the search takes the full width and the toolbar buttons share the next row; every toolbar and scope control is at least 44 px tall and the search input uses 16 px text. No horizontal scroll at 390 px and 320 px.

## Out of scope

- My patients behaviour and the demo-role row: 2.28.
- New Filter menu options (acuity, staff, no technician, no veterinarian): 2.26c.
- The tablet and phone record drawer: 2.27.
- The board table, cards, stats strip and footer: unchanged.
