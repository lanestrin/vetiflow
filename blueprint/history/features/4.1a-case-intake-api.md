## Goal

Staff add an arriving pet from the prototype's **New patient intake** dialog:
`POST /api/cases/intake` creates the case in stage Incoming with a
provisional priority, so it appears on every board as **New** and "Awaiting
triage".

## Design reference

`blueprint/reference/vetiflow-er-dashboard.html`, `#intakeForm`: patient name*,
species* (Dog, Cat, Other), breed / description, age ("e.g. 8 years"), pet
parent / guardian* (full name), phone number*, chief complaint*, initial
priority* (Critical / immediate, Urgent, Stable), triage notes, known
allergies, current medications, initial treatment authorization (Not
discussed, Pending, Authorized, Declined), referring clinic. "Arrival time is
recorded on submission. Priority is provisional until a clinician completes
triage."

## Decisions taken for this item (2026-10-28)

Recorded in the build plan's Decided list:

- **Allergies and medications** (the person's answer): kept on the patient and
  carried to the next visit; each intake also records what was confirmed at
  that visit on the case, so staff can check next time whether the pet is
  still on the same medicines and doses.
- **Age at intake** (recommended default): stored as an estimated date of
  birth, marked estimated.
- **Owner name** (recommended default): the form's single "Pet parent /
  guardian" field is split at the last space into first and last name; one
  word is refused with a field error.
- **Returning owner** (recommended default): a phone number whose digits match
  an existing owner's reuses that owner (their stored details stay); the pet is
  a new patient unless the request names an existing patient.

## In scope

- `POST /api/cases/intake` for team members.
- A new patient, or an existing one by `patientId` (for 4.7 and the Patients
  page); a patient with an active case gets `409` naming that case.
- The case: stage Incoming, status Unassigned, arrival and stage time set by
  the server; the priority through `RecordTriage` (assessed by the signed-in
  staff member, with the triage notes); treatment authorization; referring
  clinic; the allergies and medications confirmed at this visit.
- The patient: allergies and current medications updated when given; the
  estimated date of birth when an age is given.
- An `Arrived` event by the signed-in staff member; `caseUpdated` on the hub.
- New columns and the `AddIntakeFields` migration.

## Out of scope

- The dialog (4.1b), duplicate warnings (4.7), completing triage (4.2a),
  showing allergies and medications in the panel (3.2).

## Data / contracts

- **Patient:** `Allergies` and `CurrentMedications` (text, up to 500),
  `DateOfBirthIsEstimated` (bool, false for existing rows).
- **EmergencyCase:** `AllergiesAtIntake`, `MedicationsAtIntake` (text, up to
  500, what was confirmed at this visit), `TreatmentAuthorization` (enum
  `NotDiscussed | Pending | Authorized | Declined`, stored as text, default
  `NotDiscussed`), `ReferringClinic` (text, up to 150).
- **Request** (JSON, camelCase): `patientId?`, `petName`, `species`, `breed?`,
  `age?`, `ownerName`, `ownerPhone`, `chiefComplaint`, `priority`
  (`critical | urgent | stable`), `triageNotes?`, `allergies?`, `medications?`,
  `treatmentAuthorization?`, `referringClinic?`. `petName` and `species` are
  required unless `patientId` is given. Phone: digits and `( ) + . -` spaces,
  7 to 15 digits.
- **201** with `Location: /api/cases/{id}` and `{ id, caseNumber, patientId }`.
- **400** ProblemDetails with field errors; **404** unknown `patientId`;
  **409** ProblemDetails with `activeCaseId` and `activeCaseNumber` when the
  patient already has an active case (checked first, and the active-case index
  catches a race).
- Hub: `caseUpdated(caseId)`.

## Security

Team members only (`401` signed out, `403` pet parent). Owner phone and the
notes, allergies and medications are never logged or sent to the hub; error
responses name fields, never their values. The board list still returns the
owner's name only.
