## Goal

The client follows 0.4a: acuity is `critical`, `urgent` or `stable`, and the
badge shows each level in the prototype's colours (red, amber, teal), so a
Stable case no longer renders blank once the API is deployed.

## Design reference

`blueprint/reference/vetiflow-er-dashboard.html`: the detail panel's acuity
badge (`.criticalbadge`, set by `selectPatient`): Critical `#c94f43` on
`#fff0ed`, Urgent `#a46c1e` on `#fff5e4`, Stable `#078b81` on `#dff4ef`, no
border. The lane heads use the same hues.

## In scope

- `AcuityLevel` type: `"critical" | "urgent" | "stable"`.
- `AcuityBadge`: labels Critical, Urgent, Stable; colours from new acuity
  tokens; untriaged stays "Not triaged".
- New tokens in `_tokens.scss`: a text and a background colour per level.
- Tests, builders and fixtures move off `high` and `low`.

## Out of scope

- The badge's shape and the cards around it (2.11b, 3.2b).
- The acuity lanes (2.11b).

## Data / contracts

- `AcuityLevel = "critical" | "urgent" | "stable"`, matching 0.4a's JSON.
- Tokens (later items reuse them for lanes, badges and tags):
  `--color-acuity-critical` `#b9493e` on `--color-acuity-critical-bg` `#fff0ed`;
  `--color-acuity-urgent` `#99641c` on `--color-acuity-urgent-bg` `#fff5e4`;
  `--color-acuity-stable` `#067970` on `--color-acuity-stable-bg` `#dff4ef`.
  The text shades are the prototype's hues darkened just enough for WCAG AA
  (4.6:1 on their backgrounds, 4.8:1 or more on the lane background `#fafcfb`
  and on white); the prototype's own shades are 3.6 to 4.1:1. Same approach as
  the sign-in teal.
